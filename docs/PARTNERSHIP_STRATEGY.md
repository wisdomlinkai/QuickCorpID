# QuickCorpID Partnership Strategy

## Types of Partners to Target

### 1. Company Secretaries (Primary Partners)
**Why**: They serve SMEs who need CorpID, but lack digital tools

| Company | Type | Contact Method |
|---------|------|----------------|
| HSBC Corporate Services | Large | Business development |
| Standard Chartered Trust | Large | Partnership inquiry |

We've developed a bilingual (EN/ZH) platform that simplifies

Given [Company Name]'s expertise in company secretarial services,

## Partnership Terms to Prepare

### 1. White-Label Agreement
- Custom branding (logo, colors, domain)
- Data ownership
- SLA terms
- Pricing tiers
- Support responsibilities

### 2. Affiliate Agreement
- Commission rate (15-25%)
- Tracking mechanism
- Payment terms (monthly)
- Performance requirements

### 3. API Partner Agreement
- API access terms
- Rate limits
- Data usage
- Support level
- Pricing (per-call or monthly)

### 4. Integration Partnership
- Technical requirements
- Testing procedures
- Go-live process
- Ongoing support

## Legal Considerations

### For Partnerships
1. **NDAs** - Sign before sharing sensitive info
2. **Partnership Agreement** - Define terms clearly
3. **Data Processing Agreement** - If sharing customer data
4. **White-Label Terms** - Brand usage, liability

### Recommended Lawyers
- [ ] Find a corporate lawyer for agreements
- [ ] Consider fixed-fee startup packages
- [ ] HK$5,000-15,000 for basic partnership templates
